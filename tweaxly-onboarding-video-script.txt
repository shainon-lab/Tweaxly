TWEAXLY - FIRST-SIGN-UP ONBOARDING VIDEO
2-MINUTE PLATFORM EXPLAINER (plays in modal on first login)

================================================================
FORMAT
- Two tracks: VISUAL (what the viewer sees) + VOICEOVER (what they hear).
- Pace: ~150 words/min conversational. Total VO ~280 words = ~1:55.
- Tone: warm, confident, like a smart friend showing you a tool.
- No finance jargon. Plain English throughout.
- Music: soft, optimistic, low-mix under the VO. Subtle UI sound effects on clicks.
================================================================


----------------------------------------------------------------
SCENE 1 - WELCOME (0:00 - 0:10)  ~22 words
----------------------------------------------------------------
VISUAL:
- Tweaxly logo animates in on a clean light background.
- Cuts to a soft, blurred glimpse of the dashboard behind it.
- Lower-third title card: "Welcome to Tweaxly".

VOICEOVER:
"Welcome to Tweaxly. In the next two minutes, you'll see exactly
how to turn your business numbers into answers you can actually use."


----------------------------------------------------------------
SCENE 2 - UPLOAD YOUR DATA (0:10 - 0:35)  ~55 words
----------------------------------------------------------------
VISUAL:
- Cursor lands on the left-nav, clicks "Data".
- Empty state shows: "Drop a file or click to upload".
- A spreadsheet icon drags in. Subtle drop animation.
- Progress bar fills smoothly (sped up).
- Screen flips to a clean table view of categorized transactions.
- Small green check chip: "Ready".

VOICEOVER:
"Start by adding your business data. Drop in a spreadsheet, or an
export from your accounting tool. Tweaxly reads it, organizes it,
and gets it ready for you in seconds. You don't need to clean it,
format it, or learn anything new. If you can drag a file, you're in."


----------------------------------------------------------------
SCENE 3 - YOUR DASHBOARD (0:35 - 1:00)  ~50 words
----------------------------------------------------------------
VISUAL:
- Cursor clicks "Dashboard".
- Cards animate in one-by-one: Money In, Money Out, What's Left,
  This Month vs. Last Month.
- Numbers count up. A small comparison arrow turns green on one card.

VOICEOVER:
"Your dashboard is your home base. At a glance, you'll see what's
coming in, what's going out, what's left, and how this month is
shaping up compared to the last. All in plain English. No spreadsheets
to dig through. No reports to wait for."


----------------------------------------------------------------
SCENE 4 - SIGNALS (1:00 - 1:25)  ~55 words
----------------------------------------------------------------
VISUAL:
- Cursor clicks "Signals" in the nav.
- Signal cards slide in:
  * "Software spending is up 38% this month - 3 new subscriptions detected"
  * "A regular customer hasn't paid this month's invoice yet"
  * "Cash on hand will cover about 6 weeks at this pace"
- Each card has a short "Why this matters" line underneath.

VOICEOVER:
"Tweaxly watches your business every day, so you don't have to. When
something important changes - a category jumps, a customer slows down,
cash starts running thin - you'll see it on your Signals page. With
the reason, the impact, and what to do about it."


----------------------------------------------------------------
SCENE 5 - CONSULT YOUR AI ADVISOR (1:25 - 1:50)  ~60 words
----------------------------------------------------------------
VISUAL:
- Cursor clicks "Consult AI".
- A clean textarea, placeholder: "Ask anything about your business".
- Types: "Can I afford to hire a part-time designer next month?"
- Clicks "Consult AI". Textarea is replaced by a progress bar.
- A structured answer slides in: short verdict at the top, reasoning
  bullets, the numbers it used, and a clear recommendation.

VOICEOVER:
"Got a real question? Ask your AI advisor. Type it the way you'd ask
a real CFO. 'Can I afford this hire.' 'Why was last month off.'
'What do the next 90 days look like.' You get a real answer in
seconds, built from your own numbers, not a generic template."


----------------------------------------------------------------
SCENE 6 - SHARE WHAT MATTERS (1:50 - 2:00)  ~30 words
----------------------------------------------------------------
VISUAL:
- Cursor clicks "Share" on the AI answer.
- A small modal: "Secure share link created".
- Toast at the bottom: "Link copied".
- Cuts back to the Tweaxly logo + a glowing "Let's get started" CTA.

VOICEOVER:
"Need a second pair of eyes? Send a secure link to your partner,
your team, or your accountant - one click, no logins needed.
That's Tweaxly. Let's add your first file and see what your
business has been trying to tell you."


================================================================
ESTIMATED TOTAL: ~272 spoken words / ~1:55 at 150 wpm.
ROOM TO BREATHE: leave ~5 seconds of music tail at the end before
the modal CTA buttons become focusable.
================================================================


PRODUCTION NOTES
- Record all screens at 1440p, crop to 16:9 for the modal.
- Use the production app with seeded demo data (not screenshots).
- Cursor: enlarged, smooth-pathed - no jittery mouse moves.
- Captions: burn in English captions; modal auto-plays muted until
  user clicks unmute, so captions must carry the meaning.
- Skippable: include a "Skip intro" link in the modal corner from
  second 1 - first-time onboarding should never feel like a wall.
- Replayable: keep a "Watch the intro" link in the Help menu so
  users can come back to it later.
